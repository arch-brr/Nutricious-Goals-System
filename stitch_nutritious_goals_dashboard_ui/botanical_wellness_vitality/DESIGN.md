## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an organic, modern, and restorative digital sanctuary for nutrition planning, dietary logging, and wellness analytics. Rooted in the visual harmony of nature, the system translates the sensory tranquility of botanical flora, fresh springs, and sunlight into a crisp, highly functional SaaS product.

The target audience spans mindful health enthusiasts, busy professionals managing wellness routines, and clinical nutrition clients who seek clarity and calm rather than clinical austerity. The interface evokes vitality, gentle encouragement, organized control, and physical rejuvenation. 

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Minimalism with Soft Glassmorphism and Organic Tactility**:
- **Purity & Clarity:** Ample porcelain negative space frees cognitive load around meal choices and macro figures.
- **Biophilic Harmony:** Fresh botanical leaf greens (sage, emerald, mint) harmonize with deep sea cyans and warm solar accents.
- **Glass & Translucency:** Floating navigation ribbons, pill action badges, and module card states employ frosted glass overlays with subtle backdrop blurs, creating an ethereal yet grounded sense of layered space.

## Colors

The color palette centers on lush vegetation and clear ocean horizons, balanced against luminous porcelain background surfaces to guarantee AA/AAA compliance.

### Palette Breakdown
- **Primary (`#1B6B52` - Deep Emerald):** Represents foundational vitality, sustained energy, and institutional trust. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation highlights, and key heading accents.
- **Secondary (`#0E7490` - Oceanic Teal/Cyan):** Represents hydration, precision, and tranquility. Applied to secondary interactive surfaces, metric progress indicators, and system analytics graphs.
- **Tertiary (`#70A24A` - Leaf Sage/Sprout):** Accents positive dietary milestones, target completion states, and fresh daily highlights.
- **Neutral (`#202826` - Forest Charcoal):** A deep, warm organic charcoal replacing harsh pure black for all standard typography, high-contrast borders, and prominent glyphs.

### Functional & Module Color Coding
To support intuitive scannability across complex dietary features without visual clutter:
- **Daily Meal Planning:** Soft Sage Green tint (`#ECF5EA`) with Forest Jade accent (`#2D7A58`).
- **Food Consumption Log:** Gentle Hydration Sky (`#E6F4F8`) with Azure Indigo accent (`#1E6E8E`).
- **Calorie & Macro Tracking:** Warm Solar Apricot (`#FFF3E8`) with Sunstone accent (`#C26224`).
- **Nutrition Summaries & Insights:** Soft Amethyst Blossom (`#F5EEF9`) with Radiant Plum accent (`#7E4F98`).
- **Surface Foundations:** Background canvas rests on pure porcelain cream (`#F8FAF8`) with container cards in clean crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by whisper-light organic borders (`#E2E8E4`).

## Typography

The type ecosystem relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a geometric yet warm, humanist rhythm that embodies modern health tech. Its wide aperture, balanced round counters, and clear letter stems deliver immediate readability on dense data tables (e.g., micro/macronutrient breakdown logs) while retaining a welcoming, approachable personality in headline displays.

### Typographic Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Hero & Section Headers:** High contrast, deliberate negative letter spacing (-0.015em to -0.02em) to command attention effortlessly.
- **Data & Numeric Displays:** Macro totals and calorie values pair `title-md` or `headline-md` weights with proportional tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment when tallying food journals.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Used strictly for module identifiers, status badges, and column tags (`label-caps`), styled in uppercase with intentional letter spacing (`+0.08em`) to guarantee quick peripheral scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements an 8-point base grid within a responsive 12-column system, delivering breathable, rhythmic whitespace that prevents dashboard fatigue.

### Layout Model
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max layout container capped at 1360px. 12-column grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content arranges into multi-pane configurations: persistent side navigation, hero metric cards, and double-column interactive module grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) page padding. Secondary cards compress into 2-column balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer edge margins. Dense interactive cards collapse into full-width vertical stacked ribbons.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal component padding relies on deliberate spatial breathing rooms:
- Compact items (tag pills, micro-chips, action toggles) use `space-xs` to `space-sm`.
- Form inputs, nutrient list tiles, and dropdown rows standardize on `space-md`.
- Content cards, dashboard widgets, and feature panels expand with `space-lg` to `space-xl` to maintain an airy, spa-like aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy adopts a clean, layered philosophy blending ambient shadows, tinted diffusions, and frosted glass planes to mirror translucent water droplets and crisp daylight.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Flat / Surface Level (0dp):**
   - Pure porcelain canvas (`#F8FAF8`). Used for foundational page backdrops.
2. **Resting Layer (Card / Container):**
   - Crisp white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E4EBE6`).
   - Ambient botanical drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 107, 82, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
   - Subtle green tinting in the ambient shadow keeps surfaces tied to natural themes rather than synthetic grays.
3. **Floating Glassmorphic Ribbons (Navigation & Floating Controls):**
   - Translucent surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`.
   - Backdrop filter blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Boundary stroke: 1px continuous hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
   - Elevation shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.08)`.
4. **Interactive Hover & Modal Lift:**
   - Active interactive hover states shift translation by `-2px` on the Y-axis.
   - Shadow shifts to `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(27, 107, 82, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.04)`.
   - Modals and popovers feature backdrop dimming via translucent botanical forest tint (`rgba(32, 40, 38, 0.45)`) combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses physical softness, pebble-like organic contours, and welcoming curves. 

With a base roundedness index of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` for standard interactive elements), the system extends fluidly:
- **Pill Elements (`9999px`):** Used universally for primary and secondary action buttons, navigation caps, status chips, and search bars. This echoes the organic pill-style navigation badges featured in the design language.
- **Module Cards (`1rem` to `1.5rem` / `16px - 24px`):** Large dashboard tiles, meal item groupers, and summary modals adopt soft, prominent corner radii to eliminate harsh mechanical edges.
- **Inner Controls (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Checkboxes, form fields, and dropdown containers use medium rounding to preserve high data density without feeling boxy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Full rounded pill (`rounded-full`). Deep Emerald background (`#1B6B52`) transitioning smoothly on hover to a vibrant plant jade (`#14533F`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Subtle inner highlight border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill:** Oceanic cyan tint with frosted transparency (`rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.08)`), teal text (`#0E7490`), bordered with a fine `1px solid rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.2)`. On hover, fills to 16% opacity with slight `-1px` elevation lift.
- **Glass Floating Pill:** Translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, charcoal text (`#202826`), and soft outline.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutrient Indicator Chips:** Compact pill badges (`0.25rem 0.75rem`, `label-caps`) with pastel fills and deep saturated text:
  - *Protein:* Light sage (`#EAF4E8`) with forest emerald label (`#1B6B52`).
  - *Carbohydrates:* Soft solar cream (`#FFF4E5`) with ochre label (`#B35C1E`).
  - *Healthy Fats:* Light sea mist (`#E4F5F9`) with teal label (`#0E7490`).
  - *Calories:* Muted lavender blossom (`#F5EEF9`) with plum label (`#7E4F98`).

### Module Feature Cards
- **Structure:** Rounded container (`rounded-xl` / `1.25rem`) with top accent gradient slivers or pastel container baseplates.
- **Interaction:** Hover lifts card by `2px`, deepening the subtle green ambient shadow.
- **Color-Coded Variants:**
  - *Meal Planning Card:* Mint-tinted header border, white surface, jade micro-iconography.
  - *Food Consumption Logger:* Teal-tinted header border, clean progress ring indicators.
  - *Nutrition Summary:* Multi-metric radial bar with pastel track backgrounds.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** High-porcelain background (`#FBFDFB`), border `1.5px solid #DCE5DF`, rounded `0.625rem`, typography in `body-md`. Focus state transitions border to vibrant emerald (`#1B6B52`) accompanied by an outer halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 107, 82, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom rounded squares (`6px` border-radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios). Unchecked: border `2px solid #C4D3C9`, background transparent. Checked: filled with primary emerald (`#1B6B52`) featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark.

### Macro Progress Bars & Rings
- **Progress Track:** Soft bone gray (`#EEF3EF`), height `8px` with complete pill rounding.
- **Active Fill:** Smooth gradient fills (Emerald to Sprout for daily totals; Oceanic Cyan for water intake; Warm Amber for caloric budget), animated with gentle cubic-bezier easing (`cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`).
